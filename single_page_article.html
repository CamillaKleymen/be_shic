Feeling at Home When Traveling: Why Is It So Important?

Traveling always brings new emotions, impressions and discoveries. But even in the most exciting adventures it is important to feel comfortable, like at home. That is why we at BeShic create an atmosphere of coziness in every room - be it a hostel, hotel or apartment.

What makes a home truly cozy?

Home atmosphere

Comfort is not only a soft blanket and hot tea. It is the warmth of the interior, comfortable furniture and caring service. In our spaces, we think through every detail so that guests feel as comfortable as possible.

Personalized approach

When traveling, it is especially important that your accommodation meets your needs. Some people seek privacy, others - the opportunity to communicate with other travelers. BeShic has formats for every taste!

Design with national flavor

Our interiors are inspired by traditional Uzbek motifs, but at the same time remain modern and stylish. We believe that a harmonious combination of cultural elements creates a unique atmosphere.

How to create a cozy atmosphere even while traveling?

  • Take something familiar with you (for example, your favorite book or a candle with your favorite scent).
  • Choose accommodations with comfortable seating areas and good lighting.
  • Allow yourself to relax and enjoy the moment.
